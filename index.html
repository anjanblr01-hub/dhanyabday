06 · One honest note, from my heart

Before you go…
one little letter.

This last part is different. It isn't meant to take away from your day—just to say something honestly.

Dhanya,

There’s something I really want you to know. You are someone who deserves to be loved, understood, appreciated, and cared for in ways that make you feel safe and valued. I admire the person you are, the way you care for your family, the strength you carry even on difficult days, and the beautiful heart behind that smile. You matter so much more than you probably realise, and I hope you never feel like you have to be strong all the time or take care of everything on your own. You deserve someone who notices the little things, listens when you need to talk, understands you when you don't have the words, and reminds you that your feelings matter too. I know that sometimes I may not have expressed my care in the way you needed, and I'm sorry for the moments when my actions may not have made you feel as valued as you truly are. I'm not saying this to bring back anything that might have hurt you, but because I want you to know that you are important to me, and what you feel matters to me. I don't expect a perfect moment or perfect words to make everything meaningful; I just hope that, with time, I can show you through the little things how much I care. For your birthday, more than anything, I wish you peace in your heart, happiness in your days, people who make you feel loved without having to ask, and a life that gives back all the goodness you give to others. You deserve that, babaaa, and so much more. ❤️

Happy birthday, Dhanya. Keep being beautifully you. ♡
